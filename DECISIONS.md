# Decisions: #
* Multi-Step Flow: The checkout was structured as a multi-step process to mitigate cognitive overload and reduce perceived complexity. By segmenting fields into logical groups (Personal, Address, Payment), we can have higher completion rates and reduced form abandonment.
* Progress Indicator: A clear, visible stepper component was implemented to ensure that users understand their current location within the journey.
* Color Palette (Trust & Authority): The primary use of muted blue tones was a deliberate choice to align the interface with attributes of trust, security, and professionalism.
* Accent Color (Visual Hierarchy): A muted pink accent was incorporated to establish a clear visual hierarchy. This contrasting color is reserved for key interactive elements (like buttons or status indicators) to draw the user's eye.
* Typography (Readability): The Poppins typeface was selected for its exceptional readability and modern aesthetic.
* Contextual Error Messaging: Clear and non-judgmental error messages are displayed immediately after validation failure.
